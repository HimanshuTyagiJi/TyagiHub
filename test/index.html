Your rank is automatically refreshed based on the total operational score array calculated and parsed across your Google Sheets database submissions logs.

No — you can take any test without signing in. However, signing in with Google lets you save scores, track progress, and appear on the leaderboard.
Are the tests free?
Yes, absolutely. All tests and quizzes on TyagiHub are 100% free. Our mission is to make quality education accessible to every aspirant.
