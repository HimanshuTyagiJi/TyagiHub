Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the Test Center.

How is the global leaderboard rank calculated?
Your rank is based on your average percentage score across all quizzes attempted while logged in. The higher your average, the higher your rank.
Do I need to sign in to take a test?
No — you can take any test without signing in. However, signing in with Google lets you save scores, track progress, and appear on the leaderboard.
Are the tests free?
Yes, absolutely. All tests and quizzes on TyagiHub are 100% free. Our mission is to make quality education accessible to every aspirant.
