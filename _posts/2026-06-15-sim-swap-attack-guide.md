    <strong>Cybersecurity Alert:</strong>

    SIM Swap attacks allow criminals to take control of your phone number without physically stealing your phone.

  </div>

  <h2>What Is a SIM Swap Attack?</h2>

  <p>
    A SIM Swap attack is a type of identity fraud where an attacker tricks a telecom provider into transferring your phone number to a new SIM card under their control.
  </p>

  <p>
    Once the transfer is complete, all calls and SMS messages intended for you start arriving on the attacker's device.
  </p>

  <h2>Why Is It Dangerous?</h2>

  <p>
    Modern online services use phone numbers for account recovery, password resets, banking verification, and two-factor authentication.
  </p>

  <ul>
    <li>Banking OTPs</li>
    <li>UPI Verification</li>
    <li>Email Recovery</li>
    <li>Social Media Recovery</li>
    <li>Cryptocurrency Accounts</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>How Criminals Perform SIM Swaps</h2>

  <p>
    Attackers usually begin by collecting personal information from social media, public databases, data breaches, or phishing campaigns.
  </p>

  <p>
    After gathering enough details, they contact the telecom provider pretending to be the victim.
  </p>

  <ol>
    <li>Collect personal information</li>
    <li>Contact telecom support</li>
    <li>Request SIM replacement</li>
    <li>Receive victim's phone number</li>
    <li>Intercept OTP messages</li>
    <li>Take over accounts</li>
  </ol>

  <h2>Warning Signs</h2>

  <div class="discover-warning">

    <strong>Watch for these signs:</strong>

    <ul>
      <li>Your phone suddenly loses signal.</li>
      <li>Calls stop arriving.</li>
      <li>SMS messages stop arriving.</li>
      <li>Unexpected password reset notifications appear.</li>
    </ul>

  </div>

  <h2>How to Protect Yourself</h2>

  <p>
    The best defense against SIM Swap attacks is reducing reliance on SMS-based verification.
  </p>

  <ul>
    <li>Use authenticator apps.</li>
    <li>Enable account security alerts.</li>
    <li>Use strong unique passwords.</li>
    <li>Limit public personal information.</li>
    <li>Add additional security with your telecom provider.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>What To Do If You Suspect a SIM Swap</h2>

  <ol>
    <li>Contact your telecom provider immediately.</li>
    <li>Lock banking services if possible.</li>
    <li>Change important passwords.</li>
    <li>Review recent account activity.</li>
    <li>Enable stronger authentication methods.</li>
  </ol>

  <h2>Final Thoughts</h2>

  <p>
    Your phone number is one of the most important pieces of your digital identity. Protecting it is just as important as protecting your passwords.
  </p>

  <p>
    Understanding SIM Swap attacks can help you recognize threats early and reduce the risk of account compromise.
  </p>
