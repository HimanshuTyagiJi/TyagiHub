    The Three Layers of the Internet

    🌑 What is the Dark Web? — In Simple Terms

    The internet has 3 parts:

    LayerWhat it isExample
    Surface WebWhat you find on Google searchYouTube, Facebook, News sites
    Deep WebNot indexed by search — login requiredYour Gmail, Bank account, Hospital records
    Dark WebAccessible via special browser (Tor) — anonymous, untrackedStolen data markets, illegal activities

    On the Dark Web — stolen passwords, credit card numbers, Aadhar data, email lists — all of this is openly sold. Every year in India, data from millions of people leaks from one company or another — and that data ends up on the Dark Web. Bigbasket, Mobikwik, Unacademy, AIIMS Delhi — data from all of these has already been sold on the Dark Web.

    ⚠️
    Never visit the Dark Web yourself — here is why: The moment you install the Tor browser — your IP ends up on a suspicious list. 70% of Dark Web sites are fake — malware downloads the instant you land on them. In India, using Tor browser is technically legal — but interacting with the sites it opens is not. One wrong click — and your entire device is compromised.
