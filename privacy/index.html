Privacy Policy

Data Protection, Structural Compliance & Security Frameworks at TyagiHub

01 Overview
Welcome to TyagiHub, the official digital workspace maintained by Tyagi MultiTech. We value your engagement across our platforms, technical micro-tools, and learning desks. This policy document outlines our non-intrusive operations, systemic structure, and absolute data protection standards to ensure a highly secure browsing runtime.
02 Voluntarily Shared Data
TyagiHub does not force identity registration or pull back-channel tracking cookies to profile your lifestyle. Our entire network operates on optimized open infrastructure. The only segments processing data occur when you engage directly:
  • Communications Data: When contacting our system help desks via official email protocols, we retain your email address strictly to process technical responses.
  • Store Purchases: Basic purchase inputs required to manage delivery loops and transaction receipts when downloading exclusive resource models from our PDF desk.
03 Decoupled Payment Gateways
Financial data is treated under rigid operational isolations. For access to premium notes and material stores, we route requests through secure third-party billing infrastructures.
Technical Isolation: Our systems do not handle, cache, store, or log sensitive billing details, card tokens, bank numbers, or authentication passwords. Processing is completely decoupled and handled by end-to-end encrypted gateways.
04 Cookie Management & Third-Party Advertising
TyagiHub does not run internal tracking tokens. However, to keep our system resources free and scalable, we interface with third-party networks (such as Google AdSense) to serve display advertising. These verified partner engines may look at cookie metadata to parse contextually accurate configurations. Users retain full rights to block or disable cookie tracking via native web browser preferences.
05 Content Integrity & Liability Blueprint
We implement thorough internal checks across our analytical platforms, programming code modules, and educational libraries to ensure maximum structural depth.

Liability Disclaimer: TyagiHub and its parent firm, Tyagi MultiTech, offer resources strictly on an "as-available" architecture. We do not provide absolute legal liability or warranty over sudden curriculum updates or computing software script mismatches. All technical facts, solutions, and exam models should be analyzed across official standard documentations.
06 External Network Redirections
Our site interfaces may contain external outbound hyperlinks. TyagiHub does not maintain administrative controls over the network scripts, security guidelines, or operational content published on external platforms.
