Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

TyagiHub by Tyagi MultiTech is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

In Phase 1 (current), TyagiHub does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no tracking scripts, and no analytics.

Cookies

We use only one cookie: tyagihub-theme — stored locally in your browser to remember your dark/light theme preference. This cookie is never sent to any server.

Third Parties

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking service. No external scripts are loaded.

Contact

For any privacy questions: privacy@tyagihub.in
