Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I recover my password if I forget it?
No. Because TyagiHub uses true AES-256 encryption and operates 100% offline, we do not store your passwords or possess any "master key." If you lose the password, the document's contents cannot be recovered.
Is it safe to lock my bank statements here?
Absolutely. This tool is designed specifically for highly sensitive documents. Since no data is transmitted over the internet, your financial information remains strictly between you and your computer.
Will this tool change the formatting of my PDF?
No, the encryption process only scrambles the underlying byte data to secure it. Once the correct password is provided, the document will look and behave exactly as the original.
